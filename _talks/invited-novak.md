Engineering of nuclear systems requires multi-disciplinary R&D accounting for radiation 
transport, thermal-fluids, material science, chemistry and corrosion, and more. Modeling and 
simulation plays an important role in technology development, especially when considering 
nuclear’s unique licensing landscape. This talk will describe the application of exascale 
simulation to multiphysics applications in nuclear engineering, by focusing on the development 
of exascale solvers for radiation transport and thermal-fluids through the ExaSMR project 
under the Exascale Computing Project, and their integration in an internationally-used and 
developed multiphysics framework, the Multiphysics Object-Oriented Simulation Environment 
(MOOSE).
